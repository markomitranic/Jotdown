---
title: Callouts
description: The six keywords, with the label they print and one an author wrote.
---

:::note
A note written with no label prints its keyword, so the most common callout still says what it is.
:::

:::tip[Read this first]
A written label wins over the keyword's own name.
:::

:::info
Info carries the blue of the palette and its glyph.
:::

:::warning
A warning can hold more than a sentence:

- a list, for the steps that go wrong
- and **bold** words for the one that matters
:::

:::danger
Danger is for the thing that loses somebody's work.
:::

:::source[Kent Beck, Extreme Programming Explained, 1999]
A source names where a pattern in the text comes from.
:::
