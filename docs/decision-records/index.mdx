An architecture decision record (ADR) is a short note explaining one choice: the
situation that forced it, what we picked, and what that costs us. Write one when
a decision will be expensive to reverse or when the next person would otherwise
have to guess why the code looks the way it does.

Records are numbered and never edited after they land. When a decision changes,
write a new record and mark the old one superseded. Jotdown kept only the
records that still describe how it works, which is why the numbers have gaps.
