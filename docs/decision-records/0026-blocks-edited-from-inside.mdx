---
title: "ADR 0026: A project's blocks are edited from inside"
icon: Blocks
description: Why a project's own block edits its props in its opening tag and its prose in place, rather than through a form or a schema.
---

**Accepted** · 2026-10-11

## Context

[ADR 0024](./0024-content-at-runtime-code-at-build-time.mdx) lets a project
bring components of its own, drawn by its site. The editor knows the
framework's blocks node by node; it cannot know a block somebody wrote last
week. Asking every block for a schema up front would make the simplest one a
chore, and a form beside the page would hide the thing being edited.

## Decision

**The block says what is editable, by how it draws it.** The kit's
`Editable.Text` and `Editable.Image` draw a prop, and `Slot` draws the prose
between the tags. On a site they draw plain markup; in the canvas they take a
caret. `defineBlock` adds a `/` entry and a field per prop, and is optional.

**A prop is written into the opening tag, the prose by the page's own
editors.** The tag is a stretch of the file of its own in the draft, starting
after the `<`, and the block's children are stamped like top-level blocks, so
both edits sit inside the block's slice without overlapping. The block's grip
carries them along when it moves, and its source can still be rewritten
whole, which every edit inside it gives way to.

**Project code is copied into the build and runs in the browser.**
`.jotdown/components` and `.jotdown/layouts` are copied into the publication
before it compiles, as client components, with `.jotdown/package.json`
installed beside them. A layout is a component handed the page, the site and
the page's content; `docs` and `blank` are built in.

**A tag nobody draws is a stand-in in the app and an error in a build.** The
app labels it and keeps it movable; a site refuses to publish a page with a
hole in it.

## Consequences

- Any component becomes editable by swapping a `{title}` for an `Editable.Text`.
- Props are strings, since a page holds no code.
- A block inserted from `/` reads as its source until the page is saved and
  the site draws it.
- An unsaved prop edit is not part of the page's undo history.
